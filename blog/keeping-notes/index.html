On Keeping
Technical Notes

In systems research, many decisions only look obvious while their context is still fresh. Months later, the conclusion remains, but the chain of evidence behind it has often disappeared.

This blog is a place to preserve the reusable parts: how a performance problem was isolated, how an experiment ruled out a tempting explanation, and how a design survived the trade-offs among performance, area, and timing.

Write less, retain more

I do not plan to publish on a schedule or turn every attempt into a polished story. A note belongs here only when the problem and the evidence are clear enough to remain useful after the project has moved on.

The goal is simple: years from now, these notes should still be accurate, honest, and useful.
